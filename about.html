PARTS TODAY, A STRONGER TOMORROW

Sandforce Heavy Equipment Parts L.L.C – S.P.C (ساندفورس لقطع غيار المعدات الثقيلة - ذ.م.م - ش.ش.و) is an Abu Dhabi registered supplier delivering high-quality spare parts for heavy equipment, commercial vehicles, military vehicles, and industrial machinery.

Operating from our dedicated facility in Musaffah M-9, Abu Dhabi, we supply contracting fleets, quarry operators, industrial plants, and transport companies across the United Arab Emirates and the wider Middle East.

With established partnerships across verified international manufacturers in Europe, North America, and Asia, we supply both genuine OEM assemblies and premium tested aftermarket components to ensure minimal operational downtime.
