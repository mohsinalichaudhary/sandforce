Sandforce Heavy Equipment Parts L.L.C - S.P.C is located in Sector M-9, Musaffah Industrial Area, Abu Dhabi, UAE. Our trade desk and warehouse operate Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Closed on Sundays). Emergency parts inquiries can also be lodged 24/7 via WhatsApp at +971 56 786 1146.

What heavy equipment brands and categories do you support? +

We supply 41 dedicated categories including engine overhaul kits, turbochargers, hydraulic pumps & cylinders, filtration units, premium bearings (SKF, Timken, FAG, NTN), radiators, cooling fans, trailer spring brake chambers, AC compressors, and engineering plastics for Caterpillar, Komatsu, Volvo, MAN, Tatra, JCB, Hyundai, and Hitachi equipment.

Can you supply obsolete or hard-to-find machinery parts? +

Yes. Obsolete and custom-engineered parts sourcing is one of our primary specializations. With established OEM supply partners across Europe, North America, and Asia, we locate out-of-production parts, fabricate equivalent gaskets and seals, and arrange express priority air freight straight to your project site.

What are your dispatch and delivery lead times across UAE? +

Standard warehouse items are dispatched same-day or next-day throughout Abu Dhabi, Musaffah, and Dubai. Northern Emirates and regional desert oilfields typically receive deliveries within 24 to 48 hours. Emergency hot-shot delivery is available for critical breakdowns.

Simply share your OEM part number, machinery model, or a clear picture of the equipment data plate via WhatsApp at +971 56 786 1146 or email sales@sandforce.ae. Our technical sales engineers verify part fitment and return formal quotations with competitive pricing and clear lead times fast.
